# Human Mode privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Human Mode is a research exercise from the Synthetic Humanities Lab. It lets you collect sources, retain notes, and write an answer within a simulated budget and context window.

## Data used by the extension

Human Mode stores your current session and task settings in Chrome's local extension storage. This includes research questions, work orders, source URLs and titles, notes, the committed draft, simulated spending, and a record of session actions. Starting another session replaces the previous session record.

During an active session, the extension observes tab navigation to follow the research workflow and restrict navigation. During note capture, it reads the selected page's text to provide a simplified reading view. It displays retained notes while you draft your answer.

## Sharing and third parties

The extension has no analytics, advertising, or developer-operated server. It does not send your session records, notes, or drafts to the developer or an AI service. We do not sell user data.

Starting a session opens a Google search using the assigned search query. Google and websites you visit receive the ordinary requests made by your browser and handle them under their own privacy policies. Avoid putting confidential information in research questions or search queries.

Exports are downloaded to your device. You choose whether to share them.

## Controls and retention

Pause releases the browser from the active workflow. Completed and aborted session records remain locally available until you start another session. Task settings remain until you change them or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes the extension's local storage. Files you exported remain on your device until you delete them.

Human Mode uses data only to provide its research exercise and follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, [open an issue in the project repository](https://github.com/Synthetic-Humanities-Lab/human-mode/issues). Do not include private session data in a public issue.
